Vasundhara Nature Studio

Live at vasundharanaturestudio.com

70Database tables
48Automated test suites
~1 wkFirst commit to production

A production e-commerce and operations platform for a real aquarium studio. Customers shop real-time stock, book services and plan tanks with AI; staff run orders, payments and inventory from the same app.

  • Inventory that can’t be oversoldStock is reserved inside a database transaction at checkout and verified with concurrent race tests. Counter sales sync back, and unpaid orders expire automatically.
  • AI tank plannerA short wizard generates a tank plan that customers refine by chat, then turn into a studio quote or a ready-made cart.
  • Grounded AI assistantAnswers care and compatibility questions from the shop’s own species data, and recommends only in-stock products that fit the customer’s tank and budget.
  • One pricing engineOptions, offers, coupons and bundles go through a single function shared by the cart, delivery estimate and final order, so totals always match.
AI architecture

Provider-agnostic LLM layer: Groq, with automatic fallback to Gemini and OpenRouter. Server-side only, rate-limited and token-capped.
